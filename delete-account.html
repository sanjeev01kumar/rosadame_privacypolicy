ACCOUNT DELETION & DATA REMOVAL PORTAL

At Rosa Dame, we are committed to providing you with complete control over your personal data. If you no longer wish to maintain your profile, you can request permanent account deletion through this verified portal.

1. What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is finalized, the following information is permanently purged from our active systems:

  • Your customer profile credentials, usernames, and registered login details.
  • Saved profile parameters (first name, last name, phone records, and email addresses).
  • Your personal app preference records and account interaction history.

2. Data Retention & Exceptions

Your request will be fully processed and completed within **30 business days**. Please note that certain transactional records (such as past order invoices, financial purchase receipts, and shipping fulfillments) cannot be deleted and will be securely retained for a limited regulatory period strictly to comply with legal tax obligations and fraud prevention laws.

3. How to Submit Your Request

You can complete your request immediately using the secure form below. Alternatively, you can initiate this process directly within the Account Settings tab of the Rosa Dame Mobile App, or email our support desk at care@rosadame.shop with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" using your registered email address.

Should you change your mind, please note that once the 30-day processing window closes and the account data is structurally purged, this action cannot be reversed or recovered. For further inquiries, contact us at care@rosadame.shop.
