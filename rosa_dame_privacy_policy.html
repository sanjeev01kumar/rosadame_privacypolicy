PRIVACY POLICY

At Rosa Dame, we value your trust above all else. Protecting your privacy is not just a legal requirement for us — it is a commitment to treat your personal information with integrity, respect, and the highest level of care.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with us, whether through our website, social media platforms, or while using the Rosa Dame mobile app.

Information We Collect

When you interact with Rosa Dame, we may collect the following information to provide you with a seamless and personalized experience:

Personal Details

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping and billing addresses

Payment Information

Payment details are processed securely through trusted, encrypted gateways. Rosa Dame does not store or retain your credit/debit card information on our servers.

Browsing & Technical Data

  • Non-identifiable information such as browser type, IP address, device type, and browsing patterns.
  • Cookies and similar technologies to enhance your website experience and improve our services.

Mobile App Data

  • Device information such as device model, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
  • IP address and location data to provide regional content.
  • App usage analytics through trusted services like Google Firebase Analytics to improve app performance and understand user behavior.
  • Push notification preferences if you opt-in.

Why We Collect Your Information

We only collect information necessary to:

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Provide customer support and manage exchanges or after-sales services.
  • Personalize your shopping experience and offer relevant product recommendations.
  • Provide regional content based on your location (IP-based).
  • Share updates about new collections, offers, or brand announcements (only if you opt-in).
  • Analyze app performance and usage to improve our services.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for commercial marketing purposes. We only share essential information with trusted third-party service providers strictly to execute operational services. This includes infrastructure partners like Wix, encrypted payment processing gateways, shipping logistics couriers, and Google Firebase for app performance metrics. All core providers are bound by explicit confidentiality obligations.

How We Protect Your Data

  • All transactions are processed through SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encryption and secure third-party payment gateways.
  • Industry-standard security measures protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
  • Only authorized personnel with confidentiality obligations may access customer information when required.

Children's Privacy

Our app and e-commerce offerings are engineered exclusively for an adult target audience (18 years and older). Rosa Dame does not knowingly solicit or collect personal infrastructure profiles from children under the age of 13. If we discover that structural metrics for a minor under 13 have been registered, they will be purged from our active files immediately.

What We Do Not Do

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not store sensitive payment details such as credit/debit card numbers.
  • We will not use your information for unrelated purposes without your consent.

Account Deletion & Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide you with our services and fulfill your transactions.

If you wish to delete your account and remove your personal information from our systems entirely, you can submit a deletion request. Once requested, your account credentials, profile details, and personal histories will be permanently removed within 30 days, except where retention is legally required for tax or fraud prevention.

To request data deletion, you may fill out our secure Web Request Form, use the account configurations section inside the Rosa Dame Mobile App directly, or contact us at our support email below with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email account.
